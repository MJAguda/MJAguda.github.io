SKIN is a UK app that helps people find the best prices for the beauty products they already use.

## What I did

- Built and maintained the **Laravel API** behind the Flutter mobile app.
- Pulled product data from **multiple affiliate retailers**, with fast search powered by **Typesense**.
- Built user-facing features, plus an **internal admin panel** the business team uses to manage content, analytics and campaigns.
- Led the **AI-powered in-app assistant** on **AWS Bedrock**, which recommends products through conversation. I owned it end to end, from backend design to the mobile UI.
